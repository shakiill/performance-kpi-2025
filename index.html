Evidence against target

Two flagship improvements, each with a before/after result. Further improvements are listed below.

Improvement 1 — Standardized multi-environment deployment & release process

Type: standardization + stronger handoffs · Scope: CPCN, IOU.AC, e-commerce, DIU website, Daffodil Software Ltd. projects

BeforeEnvironment setup and releases handled ad hoc per project. Configuration drift between development, staging and production caused deployment failures and slow troubleshooting.
AfterRepeatable environment configuration, managed environment variables, deployment checklists and post-release validation applied consistently across projects; releases planned through whiteboarding and ClickUp sprints and coordinated with development activity.

ResultBefore, preparing and running a release could take most of a working day and often failed on the first attempt because of configuration differences between environments. Afterwards the same release goes out in well under an hour from a checklist, first-time deployment failures became the exception, and the rollbacks that previously affected a meaningful share of releases are now rare. Sprint history and deployment records hold the detail.

Improvement 2 — Dynamic adaptive-bitrate video streaming service

Type: service decoupling + automation + reuse · Scope: IOU.AC

BeforeMedia handling lived inside the main LMS: one hand-made output format, no quality adaptation for slow connections, transcoding competing with web traffic, manual re-runs on failure, and video served straight off the origin — buffering under load.
AfterA dedicated streaming microservice, separate from the LMS and called over an API, that turns any uploaded source video into an adaptive stream that the player switches between in real time.

Pipeline, per source video

  • Probes the source and builds the encoding ladder from it — renditions matched to the source resolution and bitrate, no upscaling.
  • Transcodes with FFmpeg into multiple adaptive-bitrate renditions and packages HLS / DASH segments and manifests.
  • Tracks per-rendition processing status with automatic retry on failed jobs.
  • Publishes segments and manifests to Amazon S3 and serves them through CloudFront with caching and secured access.

ResultPlayback now adapts to each viewer’s bandwidth and device, so lessons keep playing on weak connections instead of buffering. Arbitrary source formats are handled with no manual work, transcoding load is off the LMS so the app stays responsive during large uploads, failed jobs recover automatically, and the same service backs every course and lesson with no per-feature rework.
